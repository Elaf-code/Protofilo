Clinical Cardiovascular Risk Predictive Pipeline & Dashboard

Supervised clinical machine learning pipeline and interactive analytical dashboard for early cardiovascular screening.

The Problem

Clinicians require interpretable, data-driven screening tools to identify high-risk cardiovascular anomalies from multi-variable patient measurements before acute onset.

The Architecture & Performance

Evaluated multiple classifiers using R (caret). Logistic Regression outperformed Decision Tree and KNN, achieving 85.6% Accuracy, 91.7% Sensitivity, 78.6% Specificity, and 0.851 Balanced Accuracy ($p=9.58 \times 10^{-11}$).
